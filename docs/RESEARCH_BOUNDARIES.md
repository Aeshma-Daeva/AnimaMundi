# Research Boundaries

## Core distinctions

Anima Mundi explicitly distinguishes:

- serialized fields from live causal traits;
- observation from mechanics;
- operational relationships from ecological semantics;
- quiescence from terminality;
- material cessation from lineage, memory, functional, and attractor cessation;
- scheduler succession from mechanics-level cosmogenesis;
- continuity records from claims about identity.

## Primary evidence rules

- Zero events are valid observations.
- Seeds are frozen before outcomes are observed.
- Infrastructure failures retry the same task identity; they do not create replacement evidence.
- Primary 128-tick cells are never stopped because they appear sterile or uninteresting.
- Exploratory succession has a separate denominator and frozen stage-local continuation rules.
- Missing observations remain missing; they are not inferred or imputed.
- An artifact is scientific evidence only after verification and atomic canonical promotion.

## Forbidden semantic shortcuts

The following labels require separate operational definitions and matched evidence. They are not inferred automatically:

- life;
- agency or consciousness;
- species;
- cooperation, predation, parasitism, or symbiosis;
- ecosystem;
- identity;
- ontological death.

## New mechanisms

A new mechanism must include:

1. explicit causal route;
2. versioned state and checkpoint ownership;
3. conserved-resource accounting where applicable;
4. matched controls and falsifiers;
5. exact rollback and resume tests;
6. observer isolation;
7. bounded authorization before larger worlds, populations, or horizons.
